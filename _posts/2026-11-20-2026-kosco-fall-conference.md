<p><strong>72nd KOSCO Conference (2026 Fall)</strong></p>
<p>2026.11.18.~11.20., Sheraton Jeju Hotel, Jeju, Korea</p>
<p>The Korean Society of Combustion (KOSCO) held its 72nd conference this fall, marking 40 years since the society was founded. Prof. Bok Jik Lee chaired the organizing committee, and APL members worked on the conference staff throughout the event.</p>
<p>APL also presented three oral talks, one poster and two entries in the flame visualization exhibition.</p>
<ul>
<li>Oral presentation, Seohee Cho: "Emission Characteristics of Ammonia/Hydrogen Swirl Flames"</li>
<li>Oral presentation, Daeyoung Jun: "Flamelet-Generated Manifold (FGM) Modeling for Flame–Wall Interaction"</li>
<li>Oral presentation, Joon Seo: "Temperature Measurement of Methane–Air Premixed Flames Using Laser Rayleigh Scattering"</li>
<li>Poster presentation, Hyunseob Lee: "Stability Limit Behavior of Bluff-Body Stabilized Turbulent Premixed Flames"</li>
<li>Flame visualization, Seohee Cho: "NH and OH Radical Visualization of Ammonia/Hydrogen Swirl Flames"</li>
<li>Flame visualization, Daeyoung Jun: "BOS Visualization of Premixed and Non-premixed Laminar Flames"</li>
</ul>
