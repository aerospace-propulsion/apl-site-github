<p><strong>4th KSCFE Future Talent Award in Computational Fluid Dynamics</strong></p>
<p>2026.10.22., KSCFE 2026 Fall Conference</p>
<p>Dr. Daeyoung Jun, a postdoctoral fellow at APL, received the 4th Future Talent Award in Computational Fluid Dynamics from the Korean Society for Computational Fluids Engineering (KSCFE) at its 2026 Fall Conference. The award recognizes young researchers selected for their ability and potential to advance computational fluid dynamics in Korea.</p>
