<p><strong>KSAS Miryeon Academic Award (美連學術賞)</strong></p>
<p>2026.11.12., KSAS 2026 Fall Conference</p>
<p>Prof. Bok Jik Lee received the Miryeon Academic Award from the Korean Society for Aeronautical and Space Sciences (KSAS) at its 2026 Fall Conference. The award recognizes his work in training students in aerospace aerodynamics and propulsion at Seoul National University. It also recognizes his international research in hypersonic aerodynamics and high-speed propulsion, and his part in defense R&D and national space development programs, which have helped advance aerospace research and industry in Korea.</p>
