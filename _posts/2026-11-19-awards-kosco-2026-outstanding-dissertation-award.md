<p><strong>KOSCO Outstanding Dissertation Award</strong></p>
<p>2026.11.19., 72nd KOSCO Conference, Sheraton Jeju Hotel</p>
<p>Dr. Daeyoung Jun received the 2026 Outstanding Dissertation Award from the Korean Society of Combustion (KOSCO) for his Ph.D. work at APL, advised by Prof. Bok Jik Lee. The award recognizes research judged original and expected to contribute to the advance of combustion science and technology.</p>
